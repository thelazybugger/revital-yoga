Privacy Policy

How information submitted through the Revital Yoga website is handled.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, register for a program, or submit a booking form, we may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, age, selected program, and information you choose to provide in your message.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, process registrations and bookings, communicate about selected services, provide customer support, and maintain appropriate business records.

3. Payments

Where online payment is offered, payment processing is handled through the payment provider integrated with the website. Payment credentials are processed by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored directly by Revital Yoga.

4. Communications

We may use the contact details you provide to respond to your enquiry or booking and to provide information directly related to the service you requested.

5. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the website. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for payment processing, website functionality, analytics, hosting, or communication. Those services may process information according to their own policies.

7. Your Choices

If you have questions about information you have submitted through the website or would like to request clarification about its use, please contact Revital Yoga using the contact details provided on the Contact page.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when website functionality, services, or business practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact Revital Yoga and Healing Centre through the website's Contact page.
