Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Revital Yoga and Healing Centre

Our Core Commitment

Revital Yoga and Healing Centre respects your privacy. Any personal information shared through our website, registration forms, or communication channels is used only to provide our services, process registrations, respond to enquiries, and improve your experience. We do not sell or knowingly share your personal information with third parties except where necessary to provide our services or when required by law.

01

Information We Collect

We collect personal details that you voluntarily share with us through our registration forms, website interactions, and general communications. This includes:

  • Full name & contact information
  • Session registrations & bookings
  • Health/wellness intake notes
  • Inquiries & direct messages
02

How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is handled solely for business and studio administrative purposes. We use it to:

  • Process and confirm your session or workshop registrations.
  • Respond promptly to direct enquiries, feedback, or support queries.
  • Send crucial schedule alerts, booking reminders, or session details.
  • Enhance and personalize your overall wellness experience at our centre.
03

Third-Party Sharing & Protection

We adhere strictly to confidentiality standards. Your data is protected according to the following principles:

No Data Selling:

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to external marketing agencies or third parties.

Service Operations & Legal Necessity:

Information is shared only with trusted technical platforms necessary to process payments or deliver services, or when explicitly mandated by law.

Questions or Updates?

If you have questions regarding your data or would like to update your registration information, please contact our team.
