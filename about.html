Meet the founder

Priya Wadhwani

Founder, Revital Yoga and Healing Centre

Priya's journey as a yoga instructor with Revital Yoga and Healing Centre began in January 2017. Over the years, her practice has grown through deep and holistic experience with yoga, pranayama, meditation and their impact on everyday life.

In 2020, she entered deeper learning in holistic health through Reiki healing, Ho'oponopono, gratitude healing, EFT techniques for emotional balance, Love Ponopono and various NLP-based techniques. Through these experiences, she came to understand how many people experience challenges across health, wealth, relationships and career.

Her intention has always been to help people overcome their problems. With the grace of God, thousands of people have connected with her and experienced meaningful changes in their health and energy levels. She continues to find purpose in helping people and bringing value to their lives.
