Meet Priya Wadhwani

Founder, Revital Yoga and Healing Centre

I, Priya Wadhwani, am the founder of Revital Yoga and Healing Centre. My journey as a yoga instructor with this wellness centre began in January 2017. Over the years, I have gained deep and holistic experience with yoga, pranayama, meditation and their impact on everyday life.

In 2020, I began exploring deeper dimensions of holistic health through Reiki healing, Ho'oponopono, gratitude healing, EFT for emotional balance, Love Ponopono and various NLP-based techniques. These experiences helped me understand how closely our health, energy, relationships, wealth and career are connected.

I have always wanted to help people overcome the challenges they face. With the grace of God, thousands of people have connected with me and experienced meaningful changes in their health and energy levels. It is always a privilege to help you and bring value to your life.
